Questions

Asked before the walls go up.

Is Vauban live on mainnet?

Not for real funds. One empty reference vault is deployed so the code can be checked on-chain, but the contract is not audited. It will be audited before anyone should hold funds in it. Do not route real funds through anything claiming to be Vauban before then.

Does Vauban hold my funds?

The design is that funds sit in a vault you own; Vauban rules on what may leave it. The owner key never goes to the agent and never goes to us.

Is this a scanner?

No. Vauban does not try to guess whether a contract is good or bad. It enforces limits you set, whatever the agent is told to do. A scanner can be fooled by a new trick; a cap cannot.

Will it slow my agent down?

Only at the gate, and only for moves you decided are large enough to wait. Everything under the threshold lands without delay and without a prompt.

Is the fort in the animation real data?

No. Figure 1 on the general plan is an illustration of how rulings work. It is labelled as such.
